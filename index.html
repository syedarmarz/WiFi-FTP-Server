More about the app

Features

Share selected folders over Wi-Fi with FTP or encrypted FTPS. Use password access, choose what to share, and enable read-only mode when needed.

Privacy

No account is required, and the app has no ads, analytics, or tracking. Your files are shared only with devices that connect to your server.
